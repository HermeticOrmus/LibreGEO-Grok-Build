# Citation Readiness

Make claims extractable and attributable.

## Steps
1. Isolate primary claims.
2. Attach evidence (data, primary sources, dates).
3. Use clear, quotable sentences.
4. Avoid weasel words and unsourced superlatives.
5. List residual unverified claims.
