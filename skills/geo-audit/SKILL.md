# GEO Audit

Baseline AI-search readiness.

## Steps
1. Clarify the entity and primary queries to win.
2. Check crawlability and clear primary content.
3. Check claim clarity and evidence links.
4. Check machine surfaces (llms.txt, sitemaps, robots).
5. Rank gaps by severity; propose 5 concrete fixes.
